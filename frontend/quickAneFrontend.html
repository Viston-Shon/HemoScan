AI Classification

MobileNetV3Large model trained on the Chula-RBC-12 dataset (80.81% val accuracy) classifies each segmented cell crop into one of 11 morphological types with a confidence score.

🤖
MobileNetV3Large — Chula-RBC-12 Classifier (80.81% val accuracy) Each cell is re-cropped from its detected centre as a 96×96 px window with CLAHE contrast enhancement — matching the exact format the network was trained on — then passed through the model with 4-way rotation averaging for stability. The model outputs a probability distribution over 11 RBC morphology classes. The predicted class and its confidence % are displayed per cell below.
